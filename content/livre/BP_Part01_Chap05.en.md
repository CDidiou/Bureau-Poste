*(Dead of night. The village is shuttered tight, doors locked and shutters closed for fear of the bad air and contagion. The square is deserted. Only the steady trickle of the fountain breaks the silence. The Cripple is leaning against the stone basin, his stick resting between his legs. The sound of light footsteps on the cobbles makes him straighten. The Midwife emerges from the shadows of a side street, a dark shawl pulled tightly around her shoulders.)*

**The Midwife :** *(stopping, slightly breathless)*
You’re mad, Major... The Constable is still making his rounds, even at this hour, and people are watching from behind their shutters. If they see anyone about, they’ll raise the alarm.

**The Cripple :** *(in a low voice, almost warm)*
The Constable’s asleep under his porch, drum between his knees. And the villagers are far too frightened of the fever to stick their noses outside. (A pause. He looks at her.) I had to see you before everything closes for good.

**The Midwife :** *(moving towards the fountain)*
Tomorrow, the town hall's barricades will be up. They'll block the road down below. If Mélie goes into labour on Sunday, I don't even know how I'll get across the stream.

**The Cripple :**
You’ll get across. If need be, I’ll carry you over my shoulder, wooden leg first into the mud.

**The Midwife :** *(a small laugh escapes her, immediately smothered behind her hand)*
Hush, you fool... Everyone is already saying you go digging beneath the plum trees at night. Don’t give them anything else to gossip about, I beg you.

**The Cripple :**
Let them gossip! It’s all they have to do while they wait for the fever to take hold. But you... *(He takes a step towards her. The tip of his crutch slips an inch on the wet stone. She reaches out and catches him by the front of his jacket. Their faces are only inches apart. In the darkness, the silence grows heavy. The Cripple gently presses his lips to hers — a brief, feverish kiss, carefully restrained.)*

*(At that very moment, a faint metallic clatter comes from the other side of the basin. A tin bucket strikes the stone rim. A boy — the carpenter’s little lad, barely three feet tall, clutching his water pot in both hands — stands frozen in the shadows of the washhouse, his eyes wide.)*

**The Midwife :** *(jerking upright, her voice muffled)*
Jesus!

**The Cripple :** *(without moving, looking at the boy without hostility, his voice low but sharp)*
Hey, lad. What are you doing out at this hour? Does your mother know you’re wandering about?

**The carpenter’s boy :** *(stammering, the bucket trembling in his hands)*
It’s... it’s for Gran’s tisane. She’s coughing blood... Dad told me to run to the well before curfew...

**The Midwife :** *(hurrying towards the child and kneeling down to his level)*
Give me that. *(She gently takes the bucket from his hands.)* You need the water from the basin, the clearest water. *(She fills the pot in two precise movements, hands it back, then fixes him with a steady gaze.)* Take Two Brothers' Lane and keep running. Don't stop. And if you tell anyone you saw me here with the Major, I’ll give your parents something to be angry with you about, I promise you.
Do you understand?

*(The boy nods, terror in his eyes, grabs the pot with both clammy hands and scurries away. The hurried clatter of his wooden clogs soon fades into the dark alley.)*

**The Cripple :** *(watching the empty alley, a faint smile at the corner of his mouth)*
Look at him! You frightened the poor lad so badly, my dear Zephie, he’ll have spilt half of it before he even reaches the river!

**The Midwife :** *(turning back to him, nervously adjusting her bun, her heart pounding)*
It’s not funny, Major. By tomorrow, the whole lower district will know.

**The Cripple :** *(his voice softening)*
Let them talk. Tomorrow they’ll have other fish to fry, what with their registers and stretchers. Go home. It’s too cold for you out here.

*(A silence settles between them. Their eyes meet in the darkness. He gestures; she steps closer. They kiss again — longer this time, a deep but restrained kiss that seals their alliance.)*
