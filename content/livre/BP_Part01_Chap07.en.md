*(Evening falls. The edge of Mathers's plot, in front of his barn. Mathers works hard to nail heavy reused boards and pieces of batten across the door leaves to block all the gaps. The Garde Champêtre arrives by the lower path, his arm in a sling, his step a little heavy but his eyes on the alert.)*

**The Village Constable :**
Are you struggling at this hour, Mathers? It looks like a badger closing its hole before nightfall.

**Mathers :** *(without looking up, striking an angry blow with his hammer on a point)*
I close at home. The decree from the priest and the prefecture is clear: confinement, ban on movement. I apply it.

**The Village Constable :** *(looking at the wooden bric-a-broc nailed to the door, a bit mockingly)*
You apply it with good salvaged fir and very tight battens, I say. The prefect said “stay at home”, not “build a fort”. Are you afraid that the flu will pass through the door slats? Or is it the Lame that gives you cold sweats?

**Mathers :** *(stopping, hammer in the air, staring)*
Le Boiteux wanted to play tricks. Papers, papers... I barricade myself just in case. And you better return to the village before I count you among the intruders.

**The Village Constable :** *(smiling, approaching without fear)*
Oh, slowly. I'm as suspicious of the Lame's papers as you are. He came to lecture me on his land registry stories, but oh well! I saw him the other night, around the old boundary plum tree... He wasn't looking for the little creature for the prefecture, he was trying to test the waters. He's a rogue, this lame guy, he has an idea in his head with your plot.

**Mathers :** *(suspicious, lowering his mace slightly)*
What are you singing to me, Guard?

**The Village Constable :**
Nothing at all! I'm just saying that if you nail your gate like a deaf man, the constabulary or the mayor will find it suspicious and come and stick their noses in your affairs. Whereas if we get along well, I can turn a blind eye to your barricade construction site... in exchange for a helping hand in monitoring what the Lame Man is up to around here.

**Mathers :** *(surprised, frowning)*
And would you do that? And why?

**The Village Constable :** *(in a lower voice, with a wink)*
Firstly, because the Lame doesn't come back to me with his office manager airs. And then... you know it, Mathers. Even though you know I know it. I'm not blind. I know very well what you have kept under the tarpaulins since demobilization. The boxes of good iron and copper abandoned, from the mow to the twister, oh how well they sell to the city...

**Mathers :** *(grabbing the guard by the back of the collar, his voice threatening)*
Beware...

**The Village Constable :** *(serene, despite the capture)*
But I don't care about your junk! It’s not like chniole, yes! A miracle offered by the Good Lord. And you, plums for gout, you have more than you can count. I wouldn't want you to get screwed by the prefecture, you understand? In addition, it would be the entire canton that would have Pandoras in their skirts. So if you agree, I'll come back tomorrow with a real poster stamped "Infected zone - Absolute ban", on your portal. An official. With the stamp of the town hall.

**Mathers :** *(slowly releasing the guard's jacket, showing the beginnings of a grin)*
Ooh, you love them, plums! I understand that!

**The Village Constable :** *(adjusting his jacket with his good arm with pride)*
Everyone has to get along. Come on, you'll see, we'll organize this confinement for you according to the rules.
