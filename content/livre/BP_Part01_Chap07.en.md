*(Evening is falling. At the edge of Mathurin's field, outside his barn. Mathurin is hard at work nailing heavy reclaimed boards and strips of timber across the doors, plugging every gap. The Village Constable comes up the lower road, his arm in a sling, his step a little heavy but his eyes sharp.)*

**The Village Constable :**
Working yourself to death at this hour, Mathurin? You look like a badger plugging up his sett before dark.

**Mathurin :** *(without looking up, bringing the hammer down angrily on a nail)*
I'm securing my own place. The order from the Priest and the Prefecture is clear: stay indoors, no unnecessary movement. I'm enforcing it.

**The Village Constable :** *(looking at the jumble of timber nailed across the door, faintly amused)*
Enforcing it with good reclaimed pine and tightly fitted boards, I see. The Prefect said, “Stay at home”, not “build yourself a fortress”. Afraid the fever's going to squeeze through the cracks? Or is it the Cripple giving you sleepless nights?

**Mathurin :** *(stopping, hammer raised, glaring at him)*
The Cripple came poking his nose into my papers. Papers, papers... I'm barricading myself just in case. And you'd better get back to the village before I start counting you among the trespassers.

**The Village Constable :** *(smiling, coming closer without a trace of fear)*
Easy now. I distrust the Cripple's paperwork as much as you do. He's been at me with his land-register stories, but listen here! I saw him the other night, around the old boundary plum tree... He wasn't nosing about for the Prefecture's sake. He was sounding the ground. That Cripple's a sly one. He's got plans for your little patch of land.

**Mathurin :** *(suspiciously, lowering the sledgehammer slightly)*
What exactly are you telling me, Constable?

**The Village Constable :**
Nothing at all! I'm just saying that if you nail your gate shut like a madman, the police or the mayor will find it suspicious and come poking their noses into your business. Whereas if we understand one another, I can turn a blind eye to your little fortifications... in return for a bit of help keeping an eye on what the Cripple is up to around here.

**Mathurin :** *(surprised, frowning)*
And you'd do that? Why?

**The Village Constable :** *(lowering his voice with a wink)*
First, because I can't stand the Cripple and his airs, as though he were running some grand office. And besides... you know, Mathurin. You know that I know. I'm not blind. I know perfectly well what you've been keeping under those tarpaulins since demobilisation. Crates of good iron and copper, abandoned between the reaping and the little railway line... They fetch a pretty penny in town.

**Mathurin :** *(seizing the Constable by the lapel, his voice threatening)*
Watch yourself...

**The Village Constable :** *(unruffled despite the grip)*
But I couldn't care less about your bits and pieces! Now the brandy, that's another matter! A gift from God Almighty. And you've got more plums for making the stuff than you know what to do with. I wouldn't want the Prefecture getting its hooks into you, understand? And besides, we'd have the whole district crawling with coppers.
So, if you're agreeable, I'll come back tomorrow with a proper notice, stamped and official: “INFECTED AREA — ABSOLUTE PROHIBITION”, right across your gate. The real thing. With the Mayor's stamp on it.

**Mathurin :** *(slowly releasing the Constable's jacket, the beginnings of a crooked smile appearing)*
Oh, you do like your plums, don't you? I can see that!

**The Village Constable :** *(proudly straightening his jacket with his good arm)*
Everyone's got to look after themselves. Come on. You'll see. We'll get this quarantine organised properly.
