*(The boundary of the canton, marked by a bundle of brambles across the path and a sign in fresh paint: "MEDICAL STOP - DEFENSE OF PASSING". The Garde Champêtre, his face red with sweat and firmness, holds at arm's length a long rifle without a sling which he constantly readjusts under his armpit. Sitting on a stone boundary stone, a knife in his hand, Mathers calmly peels a turnip while watching it.)*

**The Village Constable :** *(taking a step back, adjusting his rifle)*
I tell you we are not passing! It’s the municipality’s decree! The tires of your machine touch the ground of the canton, so the air in your lungs touches the air of the canton!

**The courier :** *(in leather hood, trampling in front of his bicycle loaded with panniers, looking bewildered)*
But I am the flying messenger of the Sub-Prefecture! I have four urgent dispatches for the mayor, two circulars on leather grease and a letter for the receiver! You won't stop me from making my rounds! We have already counted 1,761 deaths from the flu throughout the canton, this is no longer the time for joking, old man!

**The Village Constable :**
The decree is very clear! “No one enters with a fever or mail from a contaminated area. » The city is contaminated. You come from the city. So you are a vector!

**Mathers :** *(without looking up from his turnip, with a drawling voice)*
The official is right. If you let it get through the brambles, the flu will jump on your gun and eat your butt. It's known that these little creatures like dry wood and silver braid.

**The Village Constable :** *(turning a worried eye towards Mathers)*
Shut up, Mathers! I enforce public order! *(To the courier)* Show your hands!

**The courier :**
My hands? But they're in pigskin gloves, damn it!

**The Village Constable :**
Blow into it to see if you have fire under your chin! If the mist is hot, I shoot into the air to sound the alarm!

**The courier :** *(exasperated)*
Shoot in the air? But you don't even have a strap on your firecracker! If you shoot, the recoil will send you into the ditch and your musket will puncture my tire!

**Mathers :** *(laughing quietly)*
Ah, that’s very true. Last month, he tried to shoot the grinder's hammer, the butt jumped into his chin, we had to put his teeth back in place with ironing pliers.

**The Village Constable :** *(blushing, gripping the weapon with both hands)*
It was a blast of frost in the breech! It has nothing to do with it! *(To the courier)* I order you to place your bags on the terminal, to walk back fifty steps while counting out loud, and I will come and collect the papers with a pair of charcoal tweezers!

**The courier :**
Charcoal tweezers? But you have become completely crazy in your hometown! I have to go to the presbytery!

**Mathers :**
Don't go there, friend. The presbytery was transformed into a cholera laundry room. The midwife boils mops there and the Priest cries his candles there. If you step in there, she'll soak your bag in cresyl and mow your condom down to lint.

**The courier :** *(looking at the bundle of brambles, then at the Garde Champêtre who threatens to drop his rifle)*
It's crazy... You are crazy proud! So what do I do with my bags?

**The Village Constable :**
You throw them over the brambles! And you leave by taking the big tour through the valley! Without coughing!

**Mathers :** *(getting up, wiping the blade of his knife on his pants)*
Dump them, courier. In any case, the Lame has already come back behind you to find out what was inside before you even inflated your wheels this morning.

*(The courier heaves a great sigh of annoyance, throws a bundle of tied envelopes over the brambles, turns around with his bicycle, cursing them. The Garde Champêtre cautiously approaches the bundle, his rifle trained on the envelopes as if they were a brood of vipers.)*
