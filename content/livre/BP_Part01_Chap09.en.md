*(At the district boundary, marked by a bundle of thorn branches laid across the road and a freshly painted sign: “SANITARY CHECKPOINT — NO ENTRY”. The Village Constable, his face red with sweat and determination, holds a long rifle without a sling, repeatedly shifting it beneath his armpit. Sitting on a stone boundary marker, a knife in hand, Mathurin calmly peels a turnip while watching him.)*

**The Village Constable :** *(taking a step back, adjusting his rifle)*
I'm telling you, you're not coming through! It's a municipal order! The tyres of your contraption are touching district soil, which means the air in your lungs is touching district air!

**The Courier :** *(in a leather greatcoat, pacing anxiously beside his bicycle, its panniers bulging with mail)*
But I'm the Sub-Prefecture's flying messenger! I've got four urgent dispatches for the Mayor, two circulars about leather grease, and a letter for the revenue officer! You can't stop me doing my round! We're already counting 1,761 deaths from influenza across the district. This is no time for fooling about, my friend!

**The Village Constable :**
The order is perfectly clear! “No person may enter carrying fever or mail from a contaminated area.” The town is contaminated. You come from the town. Therefore, you are a vector!

**Mathurin :** *(without looking up from his turnip, in a slow drawl)*
He's right, that official. Let him through the thorns and the fever'll leap onto your rifle and chew the stock clean off. Everyone knows these little beasts love dry wood and silver braid.

**The Village Constable :** *(casting a worried glance at Mathurin)*
Shut it, Mathurin! I'm enforcing public order! (To the Courier.) Show me your hands!

**The Courier :**
My hands? They're inside pigskin gloves, you bloody fool!

**The Village Constable :**
Breathe into them and see if you've got a fever under your chin! If the mist comes out hot, I'll fire into the air to raise the alarm!

**The Courier :** *(exasperated)*
Fire into the air? You haven't even got a sling on that old blunderbuss! You'll be blown backwards into the ditch, and your musket will puncture my tyre!

**Mathurin :** *(chuckling under his breath)*
That much is true. Last month he tried to shoot the knife-grinder's dog. The stock jumped up and caught him on the chin. We had to put his teeth back in with a pair of fencing pincers.

**The Village Constable :** *(flushing, gripping the rifle with both hands)*
That was a freeze-up in the breech! Nothing to do with me! (To the Courier.) I order you to put your bags on the marker, walk fifty paces back while counting aloud, and I'll collect the papers with a pair of coal tongs!

**The Courier :**
Coal tongs? You people have completely lost your minds out here! I've got to get to the presbytery!

**Mathurin :**
Don't go there, friend. The presbytery's been turned into a cholera laundry. The Midwife's boiling mops in there and the Priest is mourning his candles. Set one foot inside and she'll soak your satchel in cresyl and shear your greatcoat down to the threads.

**The Courier :** *(looking at the thorn branches, then at the Village Constable, who looks in danger of dropping his rifle)*
This is madness... You're a proud bunch of lunatics! What am I supposed to do with my bags, then?

**The Village Constable :**
Throw them over the thorns! Then turn round and take the long way through the hollow! And don't cough!

**Mathurin :** *(standing up, wiping the blade of his knife on his trousers)*
Chuck 'em over, courier. The Cripple's already been back through here behind you, trying to find out what's inside before you even pumped up your tyres this morning.

*(The Courier lets out a long, exasperated sigh, throws a bundle of tied-up letters over the thorns, and turns his bicycle around, cursing under his breath. The Village Constable cautiously approaches the bundle, his rifle trained on the envelopes as though they were a nest of vipers.)*
